1) Abstract

$PVBX is a bot-first, deflationary token. The trading bot realizes profits off-chain, then each realized-profit cycle is routed:

Protocol
20%
Buyback + Burn
30%
Compound
50%

Anti-Frontrunning Design: Buybacks execute at randomized times inside a 24–72h window after a snapshot to reduce frontrunning. After execution, we publish proofs (conversion, swap, burn) and a weekly report (PnL, ROI, win rate, amounts burned, compounded balance).

Once v1 earns trust and liquidity, v2 introduces the Arena (Player vs. Bot) weekly game.

3) Mechanism — Bot-First Buyback & Burn (v1)

Cycle (weekly or threshold-based):

  1. Bot runs off-chain (venue not disclosed).
  2. Realize PnL → take snapshot.
  3. Split: 20% protocol / 30% buyback+burn / 50% compound.
  4. Convert the buyback share to Solana liquidity and swap into $PVBX via best route (aggregator).
  5. Burn purchased tokens to the Solana null address.
  6. Publish proofs after execution (conversion, swap, burn) and a weekly report.

Randomized execution (anti-frontrun):

  • Burns execute at random times within 24–72h after snapshot.
  • Multiple micro-buys with jitter (random order sizes and timestamps).
  • Proofs and reporting are posted after the window closes.

4) Randomized Buyback & Burn — Execution Design

Goal: Reduce frontrunning by hiding exact timing and splitting orders.

Window & Jitter

After snapshot, choose ΔT ∈ [24h, 72h). Schedule N micro-buys at random offsets with ±J% jitter; random size split; enforce slippage caps.

Routing & Safety

Route via best-path aggregator with slippage ≤ s%; re-roll if market impact is too high.

Publication

After all micro-buys, publish the proof pack and weekly digest.

6) Economics

  • Deflation: Recurring buybacks → 30% of realized profit used to burn supply.
  • Compounding: 50% of realized profit compounds to increase future buyback power.
  • Protocol: 20% funds operations/infra/audits.

No revenue promises: Outcomes depend on markets and risk controls.

7) Governance & Operations

  • Protocol allocation (20%) funds ops/infra/audits; currently routed to the protocol wallet until a formal treasury contract is deployed.
  • Treasury today: single-signer, with a roadmap to an on-chain USDC contract that buys & burns at random times.
  • Parameters: cadence, split, Arena rules → published proposals + community review.
  • Verification: Verified community members reproduce weekly calculations and sign off via a public checklist (no attestor multisig).

8) Security Model

  • Custody: Minimal hot-wallet exposure; hardware signers; key rotation policy.
  • Contracts (roadmap): Narrow-scoped USDC buy-and-burn contract with randomized execution.
  • Ops controls: Checklists and two-person review for settlement actions and reporting.
  • Incidents: Disclose quickly; pause procedures for future v2 vaults.

9) Roadmap & KPIs

Phase v1 — Pump.fun (Now)
  • Weekly/threshold cycle: snapshot → randomized buys → burn → report + proofs.
  • Split: 20% protocol / 30% buyback+burn / 50% compound.
  • Build socials, holder base, and operational discipline.
Graduation Milestone

"Graduate from Pump.fun" = the bonding-curve stage completes and the token migrates to a DEX (e.g., Raydium), with:

  • Liquidity pool created and verified, initial liquidity added (and locked where applicable).
  • Mint/ownership configs finalized (e.g., mint authority renounced if/when appropriate).
  • Pump.fun URL updated to show Graduated status / DEX link live.
  • Public post confirming graduation + next steps.
Phase v1.b — Post-Graduation Stabilization
  • Continue randomized buyback+burn and weekly reports.
  • Observe N ≥ 2–4 consecutive weeks of healthy liquidity and on-time proofs.
  • Validate infra for the future USDC buy-and-burn contract.
Gate to Phase v2 (Arena) — KPIs

All must be true after graduation:

  • ≥ 4 consecutive on-time weekly reports.
  • Stable treasury growth trend and no open security issues.

Only after these KPIs are met do we spec, audit, and launch v2: Arena (Player vs Bot).
